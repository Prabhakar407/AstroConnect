### State rules and dangerous boundaries

- **Availability:** all bookings, holds and closures use the same database transaction/slot ownership rule. Recheck price, time, horizon and slot ownership on the server at checkout. Database clock controls deadlines.
- **Payment:** keep order-creation intent, provider order binding, hold, payment and confirmation separate. Never keep a database lock open while waiting for an external payment service. An uncertain order-creation response must not blindly create more payable orders.
- **Payment race:** browser callback, payment notification and reconciliation can arrive more than once or in any order. Verify the server-stored order, merchant/mode, amount, currency and capture state; use one finalization path. A late payment cannot displace a later booking or reopen a cancelled appointment.
- **Booking:** preserve the existing 10-minute-or-session-start hold limit until the Razorpay review establishes compatible behavior. A paid exception is visible even if it has no remaining slot claim; it must not disappear from the client's view.
- **Delivery:** preserve saved intent in the booking/inquiry transaction. Dispatch only safe record IDs, not birth details or secrets. Claim attempts with bounded leases; re-read current booking/cancellation state before side effects; retain per-provider/per-recipient results. Handle cancellation racing meeting creation and suppress stale confirmation jobs.
- **Partial success:** if the meeting exists but mail fails, reuse the same meeting. If one recipient succeeds, retry only unfinished work. Distinguish accepted email from observed delivery/bounce; do not promise inbox placement or automatic addition to every customer's calendar.
- **Uncertain success:** an external service may accept a request before our server times out. Use stable provider IDs and durable results; check before replay. Beyond a provider's deduplication window, unresolved sends need a recorded recovery decision, not a claim of exactly-once delivery. Resend currently retains idempotency keys for 24 hours. [Resend reference](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/idempotency-keys).
- **Customer recovery:** a booking ID or email address alone must not expose a booking. C03 defines an independent receipt secret, bound/digested on first save, limited status responses and fresh email verification for a single booking on another device. UUID deduplication is not authorization. Refresh/retry must recover existing checkout/status safely, not charge again or falsely confirm.
- **Closure/cancellation:** reject a conflicting range as a whole; never partly apply it unnoticed. Phone cancellation preserves the record/payment audit and queues the correct event update. Reopening only removes that closure's claims.
- **Inquiries:** acknowledge durable save, not unverified delivery. Maintain the original form purpose and retry ID, preserve typed details after failure, and make undelivered inquiries retrievable privately.
- **Prompt payment versus rescue:** the 15-minute sweep is longer than a ten-minute hold. Confirm promptly through browser verification/webhooks and bounded active-checkout reconciliation; the sweep is only failure recovery. A first verified capture after expiry enters review under C04/D06. After a long outage, suppress stale upcoming-session notices and reconcile obligations before dispatch.
- **Quota versus eligibility:** an absent Pro subscription alone never disables booking. Exhausted function/database/email limits or broken providers can; check their health separately, preserve recoverable obligations and never turn such failures into false success or an automatic purchase.

Selected task delivery must be treated as potentially duplicated or reordered; application state must provide these protections regardless of provider. Verify the chosen service's actual retry, retention and signature contracts before implementation.
